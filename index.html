Selected focus problem

Dynamite Fishing (AFA)

Root cause analysis findings

Fish bone diagram

The findings for the fish bone diagram is that dynamite fishing is caused by access to explosives and for quick income. People use it because it can catch many fish in a short time. Additionally, bad governance, weak law enforcement, and the absence of strict penalties allow this unlawful practice to continue.

Mind map

The findings for mindmap shows that dynamite fishing started in the late 1800s and sooner or later spread worldwide. Local fishermen used it because it was a faster way to catch more fish, but it’s illegal because it uses explosives to stun or kill fish and it also destroys their habitat. This has even affected areas like Thitu island in the West Philippine Sea.

5 Whys

The findings for 5 Whys is that fishermen turn to dynamite fishing because traditional fishing is too slow and catches are dropping, while access to explosives make it feel like a quick and easy catch. So, this makes them focus on making money rather than worrying about the damage done to the environment.

Proposed solution

Our proposed solution is a social media poster/infographic awareness post, that warns about the effects and the causes of dynamite fishing. This solution can help lessen the amount of dynamite fishing operators and making them aware and alerted of the damage they are doing to the environment.

Research findings

Dynamite fishing causes serious damage to coral reefs and makes it harder for them to recover. Coral reefs with uneven coral growth are more easily harmed by explosives. The study also explains that dynamite fishing slows down the recovery of damaged corals and makes them less able to recover from natural damage. It shows how harmful using explosives for fishing is to marine life and the ocean.
